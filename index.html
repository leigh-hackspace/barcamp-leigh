Format

Saturday: Unconference

Attendees present their own talks. Talks may be on any topic that fits within the code of conduct. Previous Barcamps have held talks including technology, knitting, narrative game development, and community. You do not need to be an expert or seasoned presenter to give a talk, first timers young and old are very welcome.

Attendees schedule talks at the beginning of Saturday using a whiteboard and post-it notes.

The schedule is not fixed and may change at any point during the day.

Sometimes due to the transient stickiness of off-brand post-it notes.

Saturday Evening: Social

Not yet organised, but we will be hosting an evening social in the Hackspace for attendees.

Sunday: Workshops

We are planning on using the Hackspace to hold a number of workshops on the Sunday. Workshops will be ticketed and priced separately. If you have an idea and would like to run a workshop, please get in touch. Workshops will be announced closer to ticket launch.

Capacity and Tickets

60-80 unconference tickets to be released in late December.

Unconference: free
Workshops: priced individually.
